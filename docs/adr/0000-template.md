# NNNN. Title in the imperative: what was decided

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision: the problem, the facts measured, the options seen.

## Decision

What we do, in one or two sentences a reviewer can check the code against.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what would make us revisit it.
